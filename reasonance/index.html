Ourgument

An argumentation map allows you to map what you think and why. Keep track of your values and compare them to what others think in this nuanced social app.

  • Parse out the strongest arguments for and against conclusions.
  • Consider ideas you've never heard before.
  • Record your confidence and how significant a claim is to you.
  • Share or remain as private as you want.
  • Print out maps of your arguments to present.
