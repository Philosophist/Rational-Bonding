    Alchemfighter

    Level 1: Electromagnetic Stardust

    Welcome to the System. Currently your system is 80 nanometers (nm) in diameter. That's about a thousand times smaller than a bacterium. Your objective within the system is to achieve a state. In this level, your goal is to form a neutral hydrogen atom. The edge of the system allows for photons (light) to pass, but not much else.
