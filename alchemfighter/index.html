Alchem Fighter

A space fighter where the "space" is the inside of matter. You fly at the scale of atoms, and your guns fire photons, electrons, protons, neutrons and whole atoms.

The idea

Every level is a real physical system with a goal: excite this electron, ionise that atom, turn one element into another. You win by steering the system there with the particles you fire. Skill comes from understanding the physics, not from fast aim.

  • Photon gun: tune the colour of light to lift electrons between orbitals, or knock them out.
  • Electron, proton and neutron guns: change an atom's charge, its element or its isotope.
  • Atom launcher: build molecules and start reactions.

A creative mode is planned too: a sandbox for building atoms, molecules and materials from the same particles.
