    How pieces move in 3D
    • Rook – straight along any of the 3 axes (6 directions).
    • Bishop – any diagonal: within a plane, including between levels, or corner to corner through the cube (20 directions).
    • Queen – rook + bishop: any straight line (26 directions).
    • King – one step to any of the 26 neighbouring cells. Castles along its back rank.
    • Knight – 2 along one axis then 1 along another (24 jumps).
    • Pawn – forward is both “up the ranks” and “up the levels” toward the enemy edge. Moves straight 1 (or 2 on its first move) along either, but can't capture straight ahead. Captures one diagonal step that goes forward on at least one of those, e.g. forward a rank while dropping a level, so pawns meeting head-on can take each other. Promotes on the enemy back-rank edge. No en passant.
    • White's back rank sits on the near bottom edge (rank 1, level A) with its pawns one step diagonally in front (rank 2, level B). Black mirrors this on the far top edge (rank 8, level H; pawns on rank 7, level G).
